about us

Providing Solutions, Developing Business, Lasting Relations

Mistry & Shah LLP is a home to a team of esteemed Chartered Accountants based in Ahmedabad, India. With a legacy spanning over more than two decades, we have consistently elevated the performance and success of our diverse clientele. At Mistry & Shah LLP , we take pride in our ability to blend traditional expertise with innovative solutions to meet the evolving needs of our clients. With a forward-thinking approach, we strive to stay ahead of industry trends and technological advancements, ensuring that our clients receive the most efficient and effective services available. Our commitment to excellence drives us to continuously improve and adapt, empowering our clients to achieve their business objectives with confidence and success. Since our inception in 2002, we have emerged as a prominent tax and advisory firm in Ahmedabad, offering comprehensive services across Audit and Assurance, Direct and Indirect tax, Entry Level Strategy, Mergers and Acquisition, Corporate Financial Advisory, and IT Advisory.

Our growth is fuelled by the unparalleled client service delivered by our 50+ professionals, who are recognized as leaders in their field. Mistry & Shah LLP proudly represents India’s CA firm, with a presence both within and outside the country. Our commitment to excellence has earned us prestigious accolades, including the India 5000 Best MSME Awards for Outstanding Contribution in Quality and Excellence in 2021, and the National Quality Excellence Awards for “Best Firm for Setting up Business in India and Assuring Compliances” in 2017.

Tech-Infused Success:
Mistry & Shah's Unique Edge

Our organizational culture is defined by a set of core values that unite us all. These values are ingrained across the organization, guiding our actions and decisions. Leveraging our commitment to innovation, we have integrated technology seamlessly into our comprehensive ethical and behavioural framework, ensuring that these values are upheld in our daily operations.

  1. Innovation: We thrive on innovation, continuously exploring new ideas and technologies to enhance our services and provide innovative solutions to our clients’ challenges.
  2. Focus: With unwavering focus, we prioritize our clients’ needs, ensuring that every action and decision is aligned with their objectives, enabling us to deliver tailored and effective solutions.
  3. Commitment: Our commitment to excellence is the cornerstone of our firm, driving us to go above and beyond to meet and exceed our clients’ expectations, ensuring their success and satisfaction.
  4. Teamwork: Through collaboration and teamwork, we harness the collective expertise and diverse perspectives of our team to deliver comprehensive and integrated solutions that address our clients’ most complex financial needs.
  1. Integrity: Integrity is the foundation of our firm, guiding us to uphold the highest ethical standards in all our interactions and decisions, earning the trust and confidence of our clients and stakeholders.
  2. Confidentiality: We understand the importance of confidentiality in our work, safeguarding our clients’ sensitive information with the utmost care and discretion, ensuring their privacy and confidentiality are always protected.
  3. Ownership: Taking ownership of our work, we hold ourselves accountable for delivering results that exceed expectations, taking pride in our contributions and actively seeking opportunities for growth and improvement.

As we embody our core values, our vision and mission statements serve as our guiding principles, directing our efforts towards our overarching goals. With a steadfast commitment to innovation, integrity, and excellence, we aim to redefine industry standards and emerge as a leading force in the world of finance and business. Through our vision to establish ourselves as a prominent, versatile CA firm based in Ahmedabad, grounded in unwavering ethical standards, and our mission to deliver affordable services while upholding professional integrity, we strive to forge enduring relationships and become trusted partners in our clients’ success stories.

Vision

To emerge as a prominent, versatile CA firm grounded in unwavering ethical standards. Our commitment lies in offering clients the finest, cost-effective services while upholding professional integrity and ensuring timely, excellent delivery. Through this dedication and by fostering a motivated and cohesive team, we aim to forge enduring relationships, becoming trusted partners in our clients’ success stories.

MISSION

Our mission is to establish ourselves as a best-in-class, multifaceted CA firm, bound by a strict Code of Ethics. We are dedicated to delivering affordable services to our clients while upholding the highest standards of professional integrity. Our commitment to timely and excellent service delivery ensures that we not only meet but exceed client expectations. By fostering a supportive and satisfying work environment for our team, we aim to build strong and enduring relationships with our clients, fostering trust and loyalty.

From the very beginning, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) has been ingrained in the ethos of Mistry & Shah LLP. Our mission extends beyond financial success; we are dedicated to enriching communities, preserving the environment, and fostering trust through our CSR initiatives. By embracing core values of Ownership, Innovation, and Commitment, we endeavor to make a meaningful difference in society. As active contributors to social and economic progress, we prioritize concrete actions like:

  1. Environmental Conservation: Participating in tree planting drives or recycling programs to promote environmental sustainability.
  2. Employee Volunteer Programs: Encouraging employees to volunteer their time and skills for community service activities such as mentoring youth
  1. Supporting Education: Sponsoring educational programs / donating books to support access to quality education for underprivileged children.
  2. Healthcare Initiatives: Blood donation drives /  partnering with healthcare organizations to provide medical services to marginalized communities.

Together, let’s join hands and step forward to transform our world into a more beautiful and sustainable place to live.
