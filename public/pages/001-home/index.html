Our Story

Mistry & Shah LLP is a top Chartered Accountants firm in Ahmedabad and Gujarat, renowned for its exceptional Compliance, Assurance, Tax Advisory (both domestic and International Tax), and Account Outsourcing services. As a leading CA firm in Ahmedabad with a global reach, we serve a diverse clientele across various industries. Our extensive range of services includes Assurance, Risk Advisory, Tax Advisory and Compliances, CFO Advisory Services, ERP Implementation, financial management, and Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City) IFSC and SEZ registration and compliances.

As one of the best Chartered Accountants firm in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, our seasoned team of professionals combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of international standards and regulations. This enables us to provide customized solutions that precisely align with each client’s unique requirements. Whether you’re a multinational corporation or a growing startup, entrust Mistry & Shah, the premier CA firm in Gujarat, to deliver reliable, efficient, and strategic support that drives your business towards success.

Beyond our comprehensive suite of financial services, we are committed to building proactive partnerships with our clients. We aim to understand your unique business challenges and goals to provide tailored solutions that align with your vision and drive sustainable success. With Mistry & Shah, it’s not just about transactions; it’s about building lasting relationships based on trust, expertise, and mutual success.

Established in Ahmedabad with a vision of infusing excellence through technical expertise, our CA firm has become a trusted name in India’s financial landscape. Our holistic approach goes beyond addressing immediate concerns; we strive to anticipate future challenges and opportunities, positioning your business for long-term success. With a relentless commitment to innovation and continuous improvement, we ensure our clients stay ahead in an ever-evolving business landscape. At Mistry & Shah, your success is our passion.

Our journey began in 2002 with a young, ambitious chartered accountant aiming to redefine industry standards. With the support of dedicated friends, we embarked on a mission to revolutionize accounting and auditing services. Our humble beginnings soon evolved, attracting like-minded professionals and expanding our capabilities. Through precision and innovative solutions, we are proud to have served over 1000 domestic and multinational clients worldwide. Today, we are renowned for our expertise in Domestic and International Tax Compliances (GST, Income Tax, Customs, FTP), Risk Advisory, GIFT City registration and compliances as SEZ/IFSC, ERP Implementation, RBI/FEMA compliance, and accounting outsourcing, solidifying our position as one of Ahmedabad’s most respected CA firms.

Our heritage is rich with comprehensive knowledge and invaluable experience. Our team comprises seasoned chartered accountants and professional associates who execute their work with unmatched accuracy and efficiency. This winning combination has positioned us as one of the most sought-after accounting firms in India.

At the core of our organization, we prioritize innovation, efficiency, and excellence. We specialize in providing tailored solutions, fostering business development, and nurturing lasting relationships. Our culture emphasizes unity, professionalism, and meticulous attention to detail in every interaction. As leaders in the financial industry in India, we offer a diverse range of services delivered with dedication. Our mission extends beyond client satisfaction; it encompasses a dedication to societal betterment.

Join us on our journey towards excellence and positive impact.
